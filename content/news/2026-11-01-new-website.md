Welcome! This site brings together everything happening in the lab: our **research themes**, an up-to-date **publication list**, **teaching resources** for students, and news like this post.

## What you'll find here

- **Research**: the questions we're working on, from antifungal discovery to formulation.
- **Publications**: searchable and filterable, with one-click citations.
- **Teaching**: courses, current scholars and alumni, and openings for new students.

## Adding a post

Each news post is a Markdown file in `content/news/`. Add a file, push it to GitHub, and the site rebuilds itself in about a minute.

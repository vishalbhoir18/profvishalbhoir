I presented our recent work on **synergistic combinations** that restore fluconazole activity against biofilm-forming *Candida* strains.

Key takeaways from the talk:

1. Synergy screening is cheap, fast and under-used.
2. Biofilms change the rules, so always test against them.
3. Formulation should be considered *early*, not after discovery.

Thanks to the organisers and to the students who presented posters alongside.
